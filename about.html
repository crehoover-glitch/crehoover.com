About Us

The neighborhood association for Cahaba River Estates in Hoover, Alabama.

Who we are

The Cahaba River Estates Homeowners Association is a social and recreational group run by neighbor volunteers. Our purpose is to bring people together and keep everyone connected.

The association does not set or enforce property rules.
